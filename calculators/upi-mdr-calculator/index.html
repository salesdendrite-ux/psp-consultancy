UPI MDR Calculator

In shortEnter the payment amount and the merchant category and the calculator shows the MDR, the GST on it and what you receive, using the framework that takes effect on 15 October 2026.

How it is calculated

Payments up to ₹2,000 carry no MDR. Above ₹2,000, standard person-to-merchant payments pay 0.4%, capped at ₹300 (the cap applies from ₹75,000). Specified sectors such as railways, telecom, insurance, fuel, agricultural inputs and utility bills pay a flat ₹5. Mutual funds, securities and stockbrokers pay 0.02%, capped at ₹300. Small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month through UPI QR into a bank account are exempt.

GST at 18% is reported to apply to MDR and is shown as an option. Confirm the rate and the final charge on your acquiring bank's invoice. Read the full explainer: UPI MDR 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How is UPI MDR calculated?

For a standard merchant payment above ₹2,000, MDR is 0.4% of the payment value, capped at ₹300 per transaction. For example, ₹10,000 attracts ₹40 and ₹1,00,000 attracts ₹300.

When does UPI MDR start?

From 15 October 2026, on eligible person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000.

Do customers pay UPI MDR?

No. MDR is a merchant-side charge, and merchants cannot pass it on to customers as a separate UPI charge.
