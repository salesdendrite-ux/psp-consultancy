FEMA Advisory: FDI, Overseas Investment, ECB and Export-Import

In shortPSP Consultancy advises Indian businesses, foreign investors and NRIs on the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999: foreign investment reporting, overseas investment, external commercial borrowings, export and import compliance, NRI remittances, and compounding of past contraventions. Where a Chartered Accountant's certificate is required, PSP & Associates issues it.

What we do

FDI inflows: structuring and reporting

Eligibility by sector and entry route, pricing compliance, and the reporting that follows an allotment or transfer of shares, filed through RBI's reporting portal with your authorised dealer bank.

Overseas investment

Structuring and reporting for Indian entities and residents investing abroad, including the annual reporting that follows.

External commercial borrowings

Eligibility, permitted end-use, loan registration and the periodic returns that follow.

Export-import compliance

Export Declaration Forms, tracking of realisation periods, extension requests and bank liaison under the unified export-import regulations in force from 1 October 2026.

NRI investments, property and remittances

Account types, investment routes, sale of property, repatriation within the limits and the tax compliance that must accompany it.

Annual return on foreign liabilities and assets

Preparing and filing the annual return that companies with foreign investment must submit to RBI.

Compounding and regularisation

Assessing late or missed filings, preparing the application to RBI and presenting facts clearly, with the late submission fee where it applies.

Remittance certification support

Preparing the information for foreign remittances. The Chartered Accountant's certificate that some remittances require is issued through PSP & Associates.

Frequently asked questions

What is FEMA and who does it affect?

The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 regulates foreign exchange transactions in India. It affects companies receiving or making foreign investment, exporters and importers, borrowers from overseas lenders, and individuals who hold or move money across borders, including NRIs.

What are the timelines for reporting foreign investment?

Reporting of an allotment of shares to a foreign investor is typically required within 30 days, and reporting of a transfer of shares typically within 60 days. RBI revises these requirements from time to time, so we confirm the current rule before filing.

What changed for exporters from 1 October 2026?

A unified set of export-import regulations took effect on 1 October 2026, with a 9-month period to realise export proceeds in most cases. Our article on the new regulations explains the changes and gives a checklist.

What is compounding under FEMA?

Compounding is a process under which RBI can settle a contravention on application, usually for a monetary payment, without a prosecution. It is used for late filings and other procedural breaches, and a clean, early application helps.

Can NRIs repatriate the sale proceeds of property in India?

Yes, within the limits and conditions that apply to the type of account and the property, and subject to tax being paid and certified. Currently NRO balances can generally be repatriated up to USD 1 million per financial year. We confirm the current position for your case.

Do you handle applications to RBI?

Most requests go through your authorised dealer bank. We prepare the application, the explanation and the supporting documents, and coordinate with the bank. Certificates that must come from a Chartered Accountant are issued through PSP & Associates.
