Frequently asked questions

Which entity should a foreign company choose in India?

Most foreign investors use a private limited company, usually a wholly owned subsidiary, because it is straightforward for investment and exit. An LLP suits some professional and service businesses, while branch and liaison offices need approvals and have limited permitted activities. The right choice depends on the sector, funding and tax position.

Is a resident director mandatory?

Yes. A company must have at least one director who has stayed in India for at least 182 days during the year, as the Companies Act specifies, and an LLP must have at least one designated partner resident in India.

How long does incorporation take?

Incorporation itself can be quick when documents are ready. Overall timelines depend on name approval, apostille or notarisation of foreign documents, and bank account opening, so we plan the whole sequence with you.

What must be reported to RBI after receiving foreign investment?

The allotment of shares to the foreign investor is reported through RBI's portal, typically within 30 days of allotment, and the company later files an annual return on foreign liabilities and assets. We handle both.

Can an NRI or foreign national start a company in India?

Yes, subject to the sector rules and the FDI policy. Foreign nationals can be directors or shareholders, and we help with documents, the resident director requirement and registrations.
