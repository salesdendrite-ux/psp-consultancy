Virtual CFO Services

In shortA Virtual CFO gives a growing business senior finance leadership on a monthly retainer: reliable MIS, budgets and forecasts, cash-flow control, fundraising readiness and a point of contact for lenders, investors and auditors. PSP Consultancy provides this alongside the tax, GST and FEMA expertise that most finance heads have to chase separately.
Talk to us about Virtual CFO

What we do

Monthly MIS and dashboards

A consistent monthly pack covering profit and loss, balance sheet, cash and the three or four numbers that run your business, delivered by a fixed working day.

Budgeting and forecasting

Annual budgets, rolling forecasts and scenario views that show what a hire, a price change or a delayed collection does to cash.

Cash-flow and working-capital management

Receivables and payables discipline, collection follow-up, inventory cover and a 13-week cash view where it is needed.

Unit economics and pricing

Margin by product, customer or project, and pricing and discount guardrails based on actual cost.

Fundraising and lender readiness

Financial models, investor reporting, a clean data room and answers to diligence questions, including tax, GST and FEMA readiness.

Board and investor reporting

Monthly or quarterly packs in a format investors recognise, with variance commentary.

Controls and finance processes

Approval limits, month-end close routines, document trails and simple SOPs that survive staff changes.

Compliance calendar oversight

A single calendar of tax, GST, payroll and regulatory dates, owned by someone, with reminders before each deadline.

Who this is for

  • Startups after their first institutional round
  • Owner-managed businesses crossing the point where a bookkeeper is no longer enough
  • Family businesses planning succession or outside capital
  • Indian subsidiaries of foreign companies
  • Real estate and manufacturing businesses with project or plant-level reporting

Frequently asked questions

What is a Virtual CFO?

A Virtual CFO, also called a fractional or outsourced CFO, is an experienced finance professional who provides CFO-level support part-time, usually on a monthly retainer, instead of an in-house full-time hire.

How is a Virtual CFO different from an accountant?

An accountant records and reports what happened and files returns. A Virtual CFO uses that information to plan: budgets, forecasts, cash management, pricing, fundraising and board reporting.

When should a business consider a Virtual CFO?

Typical triggers are preparing to raise capital, cash tightness despite profits, multiple entities or locations, lender or investor reporting requirements, and an owner who spends too much time on finance.

How is the fee decided?

By scope: size of the business, number of entities, reporting frequency and the level of involvement. We quote a fixed monthly fee in writing before any work begins.

Do you work with our existing accountant and software?

Yes. We work in your existing accounting software and with your current team, and improve processes gradually instead of replacing everything at once.
