Audit and Assurance, through PSP & Associates

In shortStatutory audit, tax audit, internal audit, RERA audit and forensic or management reviews are carried out by PSP & Associates, our separate affiliate Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. PSP Consultancy coordinates the engagement. Audit reports are signed only by PSP & Associates.
Talk to us about Audit & Assurance

What we do

Statutory audit

Audit of companies and LLPs as required by the Companies Act, 2013 and the LLP Act, 2008.

Tax audit

Audit of the books of businesses and professionals whose turnover or receipts exceed the prescribed limits, with the report filed in the form the tax law requires.

Internal audit

Risk-based review of processes, controls and compliance, with a written report and recommendations for management.

RERA audit

Annual audit and certification for real estate projects registered under RERA, in the authority's format.

Forensic and fraud review

Fact-finding on suspected irregularities, with documentation suitable for management, lenders or counsel.

Management and process audit

Review of operating processes, cost controls and reporting quality, with practical improvement steps.

Financial due diligence support

Reviewing financial statements and compliance history for investors, buyers and lenders.

Who this is for

  • Companies and LLPs that need a statutory auditor
  • Businesses and professionals above the tax audit limits
  • Real estate promoters needing annual RERA certification
  • Management teams that want an independent internal review
  • Investors and lenders needing financial due diligence

Frequently asked questions

Who signs the audit report?

Only PSP & Associates. It is a Chartered Accountancy firm registered with ICAI. PSP Consultancy is not an audit firm and does not sign audit reports or certificates.

Is PSP Consultancy an audit firm?

No. PSP Consultancy is a separate advisory practice. It helps you scope the work, gather documents and follow up, while the audit itself is performed by PSP & Associates.

Can the same group audit us and also advise us?

Independence rules under the Companies Act and ICAI's Code of Ethics restrict certain other services to an audit client. We assess this at the start of every engagement and structure who does what accordingly.

What is a tax audit and who needs one?

A tax audit is an audit of the books of a business or professional whose turnover or receipts exceed the limits prescribed in the tax law, or who is otherwise covered. We confirm the thresholds for your facts before the due date.

How do we get started?

Contact us with a short description of the entity and the year. We confirm the scope and arrange for PSP & Associates to share its engagement terms.
