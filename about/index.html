About PSP Consultancy

In shortPSP Consultancy is a business and financial advisory practice in Ahmedabad that combines tax, regulatory and finance expertise for founders, developers, exporters, NRIs and growing businesses. It works alongside, but is separate from, the Chartered Accountancy firm PSP & Associates.

What PSP stands for

Precise Execution. Meticulous attention to filings, deadlines and documentation, with a zero-tolerance approach to regulatory error. Personalised Service. Every engagement starts with your situation, not a template. Strategic Insight. Forward-looking financial and regulatory thinking, so compliance work also informs the decisions ahead of you.

Vision and mission

To be a trusted advisor to businesses that need dependable compliance and sound financial judgement, delivered with clarity, accountability and respect for the client's time.

Two practices, one standard

PSP Consultancy advises on income tax, GST, FEMA, RERA, Virtual CFO, outsourcing and India business entry. PSP & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and performs statutory audit, tax audit and the certificates that the law reserves for Chartered Accountants. We keep the two clearly separate so that each client knows who is responsible for what.

Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between PSP Consultancy and PSP & Associates?

They are two separate, affiliated practices. PSP Consultancy is an advisory practice. PSP & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy firm registered with ICAI that signs audit and certification reports. Each is responsible for its own work.

Who is CA Parag Panjwani?

CA Parag Panjwani is the founder of PSP & Associates. He provides taxation, audit and compliance advisory to PSP Consultancy as and when required. He has worked across automotive, technical textile, machinery, pharma and IT businesses as Virtual CFO, internal auditor, statutory auditor and management consultant.
