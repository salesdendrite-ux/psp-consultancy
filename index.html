Income Tax

PSP Consultancy helps individuals, professionals, HUFs, firms and companies file accurate income tax returns, plan tax legally, manage advance tax and TDS, and respond to departmental notices. Tax audit reports and other certificates that the law reserves for Chartered Accountants are issued by our affiliate firm, PSP & Associates.

GST

PSP Consultancy handles GST registration, monthly and annual return filing, input tax credit reconciliation, e-invoicing and e-way bill advice, refunds and replies to notices for businesses in Gujarat and across India. We work from your books and keep the filing calendar so nothing is missed.

Read more

RERA Advisory

PSP Consultancy supports developers, promoters and real estate agents with RERA project registration, periodic project updates, annual filings, escrow-account compliance and compliance reviews, so projects stay within the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and the rules of the state authority, including Gujarat RERA.

Read more

FEMA Advisory

PSP Consultancy advises Indian businesses, foreign investors and NRIs on the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999: foreign investment reporting, overseas investment, external commercial borrowings, export and import compliance, NRI remittances, and compounding of past contraventions. Where a Chartered Accountant's certificate is required, PSP & Associates issues it.

Read more

Audit & Assurance

Statutory audit, tax audit, internal audit, RERA audit and forensic or management reviews are carried out by PSP & Associates, our separate affiliate Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. PSP Consultancy coordinates the engagement. Audit reports are signed only by PSP & Associates.

Virtual CFO

A Virtual CFO gives a growing business senior finance leadership on a monthly retainer: reliable MIS, budgets and forecasts, cash-flow control, fundraising readiness and a point of contact for lenders, investors and auditors. PSP Consultancy provides this alongside the tax, GST and FEMA expertise that most finance heads have to chase separately.

Read more

Outsourcing

PSP Consultancy runs the finance back office for businesses that would rather not: bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, bank reconciliation, payroll with statutory deductions and filings, and a monthly MIS. Books stay GST-ready and audit-ready, and tax and compliance questions are answered by the same team.

India Business Entry

PSP Consultancy helps foreign companies and founders enter India: choosing between a private limited company, LLP, subsidiary or branch, completing incorporation and registrations, reporting the foreign investment to RBI, and putting first-year tax, GST and ROC compliance in place.

About PSP Consultancy

What is PSP Consultancy?

PSP Consultancy is a business and financial advisory practice headquartered in Ahmedabad, India. It advises on income tax, GST, FEMA, RERA, Virtual CFO services, accounting and payroll outsourcing, and India business entry.

Is PSP Consultancy a Chartered Accountancy firm?

No. PSP Consultancy is an independent advisory practice. PSP & Associates is the separate, affiliated Chartered Accountancy firm registered with ICAI. Audit, tax audit and certification work is carried out by PSP & Associates.

Where is PSP Consultancy located?

The headquarters is in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, with affiliated offices in Mumbai, Delhi, Jaipur and Indore. Many engagements, including NRI and cross-border work, are handled remotely.

Who does PSP Consultancy work with?

Founders, developers, exporters, NRIs, foreign companies entering India, and growing businesses that need tax, compliance and finance support in one place.

How do I start working with PSP Consultancy?

Contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp with a short description of what you need. We confirm the scope and the fee in writing before any work begins.
